# Emergency Room Analytics Dashboard

## Business Objective
Analyze emergency room performance to identify patterns in patient volume, wait times, and operational bottlenecks to support data-driven decision making.

## Dataset
Emergency Room operational data including patient visits, arrival times, wait times, age groups, and visit outcomes.

## Tools Used
- Power BI / Excel
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- KPI Development
- Dashboard Design

## Key Metrics
- Total ER Visits
- Average Wait Time
- Patients by Age Group
- Visit Volume by Time of Day
- Admission vs Discharge Rate
