# HR Analytics Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Overview
This project presents an Excel-based HR Analytics Dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition and workforce demographics. The dashboard provides actionable insights to support data-driven HR decision-making.

---

## Business Objective
To identify key attrition drivers across departments, job roles, education levels, age groups, and marital status, helping HR teams improve retention strategies and workforce planning.

---

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel  
- Pivot Tables  
- Calculated Fields  
- Slicers & Interactive Filters  
- Data Visualization & Dashboard Design

---

## Key Metrics
- Total Employees  
- Active Employees  
- Attrition Count  
- Attrition Rate  
- Average Age  
- Job Satisfaction Rating  

---

## Analysis Dimensions
- Department  
- Job Role  
- Education Level  
- Gender  
- Age Group  
- Marital Status  

---

## Key Insights
- Attrition rate is approximately 16%, indicating moderate employee turnover.
- Higher attrition observed among specific job roles such as Sales Executive and Laboratory Technician.
- Employees aged 25–34 show the highest attrition count.
- Employees with Bachelor’s degrees represent the largest attrition group.
- Single employees show higher attrition compared to married employees.

---

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for high-level metrics
- Interactive slicers for gender, department, and education field
- Visual breakdowns by age group, marital status, and job role
- Clean, executive-style layout for easy interpretation

---

## Outcome
This dashboard demonstrates strong foundational skills in data cleaning, analysis, and visualization using Excel. It highlights the ability to translate raw HR data into meaningful business insights.
